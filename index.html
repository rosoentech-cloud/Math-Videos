Use these videos as study resources

These lessons support your practice. They work best when you:

  • Try each example on paper before you watch the worked solution.
  • Pause and replay any step you don't follow.
  • Review the video that matches the topic you are studying, then practice more problems from your course materials.
  • Bring any questions you can't answer to your instructor.

    These videos have no narration, so read the formulas and steps on screen as they appear.
